PROJECT CONTEXT: TECHSQUARE AGENCY
We are converting this solo developer template into a 4-person B2B tech and creative agency website. Do not use technical jargon (e.g., React, Node, Docker) in client-facing text. Focus on business outcomes.

THE 7-SECTION ARCHITECTURE:

Navbar: Links to Services, Portfolio, Team, About, Blog. "Book a Consultation" button on the right.

Hero Section: 3D landing view pitching custom software, AI automation, and creative media.

Services (Replaces 3D Keyboard): Web Development, AI Automation, Photo/Video Editing, AI Ads.

The 4 Founders: Vertical timeline gateway to individual portfolios.

Agency Portfolio: Filterable grid showing our multimedia work.

About Section: Text section near the footer explaining our mission.

Contact Drawer: Pop-up routing to our shared backend.
